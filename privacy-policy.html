Datenschutzerklärung / Privacy Policy

CARASTO Soft ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile applications and the website carastosoft.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not operate our own servers for our apps and do not collect any personal identification information directly. Settings and cached data are stored only on your device. Our apps use the following services:

  • Apple App Store: App downloads, in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive any payment data. Apple provides us with anonymized sales reports and crash reports.
  • Google AdMob: Our apps may display advertisements provided by Google Ireland Limited. Depending on your consent, AdMob uses identifiers (such as the IDFA on iOS) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Consent is requested in the app and can be changed at any time via "Privacy Settings". Subscribers of CClocks Pro do not see ads.
  • Location (CClocks): Sunrise, sunset and moon times are calculated on your device; your location does not leave the device for this purpose. The place name shown in widgets (e.g. "Berlin") is determined via Apple Maps.
  • Weather (CClocks): To show the weather, your approximate location is sent to Apple Weather (Apple WeatherKit) solely to retrieve the forecast. According to Apple, the location is not associated with personally identifiable information and is not tracked across requests.
  • Ratings: The rating prompt in our apps is provided by Apple. We do not receive any personal data from it.

2. Log Data and Web Hosting

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub automatically collects standard log files (IP address, browser type, time of access) to ensure the technical stability and security of the website. The fonts used on this website are hosted locally; no connection to Google is established.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email: hello@carastosoft.com.
